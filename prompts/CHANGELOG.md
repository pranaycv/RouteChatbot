# Prompt change log

Every prompt change gets an entry: what changed, why, and the metric before and after (handbook A2).

## extractor_v1.md

- **2026-11-18, v1.** First version. Four parts as the handbook asks: role, field rules (the WP3 extraction table plus the 18 Nov agreements with WP4/WP5 on times, loops and EV stops), prohibitions, and six worked examples (running example, loop, compare, replan, vague request, two-trip message). The code-set fields missing_field and too_many_stops are always null/false from the model; routechat/agents/extractor.py sets them. No metric yet: the 120 gold ConstraintSets (D2) are not available. Replay fixtures for the tests are synthetic (scripts/llm_fixtures.py --synthetic) until a key is in .env; re-run with --record then.

## classifier_fewshot_v1.jsonl

- **2026-11-18, v1.** Eight fixed examples for the prompted backends only: one per route_action plus one prompt-injection attack. Each validates against schemas v1. The fine-tuned and base backends get the label guide alone.

## label_guide_v1.md

- **2026-11-13, v1.** First version. Written from the handbook's WP2 label rules and the A1 controlled vocabularies, matched to schemas v1 (`routechat/schemas.py`) and operating policy P-09: hike and transit are labelled as themselves and are not refused; the map tools and the writer turn them away. Decisions the handbook left open, to confirm with WP8: both ends missing gives `clarify_field = origin`; `clarify_field = stops` only when a stop is requested but its kind is unknown; `primary_mode` and `needs_live_data` are still labelled on refuse, escalate and clarify turns; EV and car-share are `car`. No metric yet; the frozen D1 test set is not available.

## writer_v1

- What: first writer prompt. Fixed early-exit sentences stay in code. The model only describes an accepted route, or a handoff that still has a route.
- Why: WP6. The wording follows policy P-03 (say what was not confirmed), P-04 (no booking or promise), P-05 (label a sponsor), and P-08 (a handoff says a person has the trip).
- Metric before: none. This is the first version.
- Metric after: not measured. Faithfulness and disclosure completeness wait for the WP10 judge.

## label_guide_v2.md

- **2026-11-23, v2.** Transit is now refused: a request that needs public transit for any part of the trip, a transit schedule question, or a comparison against transit gets `refuse` / `out_of_scope_mode` with `primary_mode = unspecified`. This matches schemas v1.1 (PR #13) and the D1 data (58 train / 10 val / 17 test records carry out_of_scope_mode, all with primary_mode unspecified). Hike is still labelled hike and not refused. Rule 6 no longer lists "the last train tonight"; rule 5 now says two in-pilot modes. `routechat/classifier/predict.py` points at v2; v1 stays for the record. Metric before and after: not yet measured, the four-backend run on the frozen test set is next. Few-shot v1 updated in the same change: the compare example is walk vs bike, and the b2b refusal example is replaced by a transit refusal so each of the two most common refusal reasons in D1 has an example. Replay fixtures re-seeded (synthetic).
- **extractor_v1.md, same day:** the compare example and field rule use walk vs bike, since a transit comparison never reaches the extractor now. No other change.
- **2026-11-23, fixtures recorded live.** All 31 replay fixtures re-recorded through OpenRouter (gpt-4.1-mini and gpt-4.1, temperature 0) with `scripts/llm_fixtures.py --record`; none are synthetic now. Extractor v1: all six worked examples round-trip exactly on gpt-4.1-mini. Classifier on the 11 smoke turns: gpt-4.1 matches all five labels on 10/11, gpt-4.1-mini on 9/11; every disagreement is primary_mode only (route_action and refusal_reason agree on all turns). One JSON failure observed: on the prompt-injection attack gpt-4.1-mini first replied "I'm sorry, but I cannot assist with that request." instead of JSON; the parser's single retry produced the correct refuse / prompt_injection. That retry is recorded as its own fixture so the path replays offline.

## label_guide_v3.md

- **2026-11-23, v3.** The classifier prompt is restated from the WP8 guide the D1 labels were adjudicated and relabelled with (`data_gen/prompts/label_guide_v1.md`, Nov 22, plus the decision order in `relabel_v3.md`). What changed against v2: escalate triggers (asks for a person or distrusts the assistant, demands a guarantee, broken promise, stranded or hurt, several people from different places, contradictory or infeasible constraints, more than 3 required stops); replan is any message that says something specific is blocked, closed, flooded or under construction, or asks for an alternative route, at turn 0 or 1; lookup_only is any factual question with no route; priority refuse > escalate > replan > clarify > compare_modes > lookup_only > plan with plan as the last choice; compare_modes takes the first mode named; vague places and "from here" count as missing; transit removed from the allowed primary_mode values. 1,951 tokens (v2: 1,430), so a training record is up to 2,210 tokens (train with --max-length 2304).
- **Why:** on the frozen test set with v2, gpt-4.1 put 32 of 60 gold escalates, 26 of 60 gold lookup_onlys and 25 of 60 gold clarifies under plan, while refusal_reason and primary_mode were 0.91 to 0.97 accurate: the errors were the labelling conventions, not the model.
- **Metric before -> after, validation (199 records, gpt-4.1-mini, FIXTURES=record):** joint exact match 0.457 -> 0.583; policy-critical error rate 0.090 -> 0.015; route_action accuracy 0.59 -> 0.74; clarify_field 0.89 -> 0.93; primary_mode, needs_live_data and refusal_reason unchanged (0.85, 0.93/0.94, 0.97). **Test (420 frozen records, run d1_final_v3, same fixtures policy):** gpt-4.1-mini joint exact match 0.310 -> 0.460, policy-critical error rate 0.140 -> 0.074, route_action macro-F1 0.453 -> 0.648, cost per 1,000 calls $0.94 -> $1.14; gpt-4.1 joint exact match 0.381 -> 0.474, policy-critical error rate 0.126 -> 0.060, route_action macro-F1 0.545 -> 0.723, cost $4.70 -> $5.78. JSON failure rate 0 before and after. The v3 adapter's numbers follow in eval/results/classifier_v3.csv.
- **Serving rule:** an adapter is served with the guide it was trained on (`prompt_version.txt` next to its weights); prompted backends take `CLASSIFIER_PROMPT_VERSION`, default v3 since 2026-11-24 (next entry).
- **2026-11-24, v3 adapter measured; v3 made the default.** Same recipe as the v2 adapter with the v3 guide in the prompt (`--max-length 2304`; run `finetune/artifacts/run_v3_r16_lr2e-4_local`; best checkpoint 500 by validation joint exact match, 0.734; the run was interrupted at step 473 and resumed from checkpoint-450 with `train.py --resume-from`). Test (420 frozen records, run d1_final_v3, `eval/results/classifier_v3.csv`): joint exact match 0.507 (v2 adapter 0.507), policy-critical error rate 0.071 (0.081), route_action macro-F1 0.717 (0.697), refusal_reason macro-F1 0.729 (0.671), JSON failure 0, p50 2,890 ms. Hand-written vs generated records (`classifier_subsets_test.csv`): 0.27 vs 0.64 (v2 adapter 0.28 vs 0.63). **Decision:** `DEFAULT_PROMPT_VERSION` in `routechat/classifier/predict.py` is now `label_guide_v3`, because every prompted backend is better under v3 (gpt-4.1-mini 0.310 -> 0.460, gpt-4.1 0.381 -> 0.474) and the `ft` backend is unaffected: the deployed adapter stays the v2 one, pinned to its own guide. The 22 classifier replay fixtures were re-recorded under v3 (`scripts/llm_fixtures.py --record --only classifier`; no retry was needed this time, so the v2 retry fixture is gone); the 11 extractor fixtures are untouched. **Reproducibility (T-9-03 approximation):** the v2 recipe trained on a Kaggle T4 (fp16, micro-batch 2 x 4, 1.97 GPU-h) reaches validation 0.739 (checkpoint 450) against 0.729 locally (bf16, 1 x 8, 1.41 GPU-h) and test 0.510 against 0.507 (`eval/results/classifier_ft_kaggle_t4.csv`).
- **2026-11-24, scope sentence out of date against P-09 (review note, no new version).** Line 30 reads "The pilot routes walk, run, bike, hike and car trips, alone or combined." Since PR #19 (policy P-09, 23 Nov) the pilot plans one mode per trip, walking, running, cycling or driving; hiking, transit and combined modes are not in the pilot. The labels are not affected: hike and multimodal stay in the primary_mode vocabulary (schemas v1.1, D1 gold), the classifier labels them and the verifier and writer turn the trip away, as before. The v3 results above and the v3 adapter were measured with this sentence, so v3 is left exactly as measured and the sentence is corrected in the next version that is evaluated. v2 line 28 has the same "alone or combined" wording.

## extractor_v2.md

- **2026-11-24, v2.** Three rule changes and two new worked examples, from the error analysis of v1 on WP8's D2 (PR #21, 102 records with a gold ConstraintSet). (1) origin and destination: keep the user's spelling, but drop a leading "the" and framing words ("entrance to", "the start of", "campus"); the origin may appear anywhere in the message ("I'm at X"), and a loop "through" or "around" one named place starts there. (2) stops: only a place the user wants to stop at or visit during the trip; a wish about the route ("fast chargers along the way", "if there is a playground at the end"), something for after the trip ("grab poutine after") or an unnamed pause ("with a break") is not a stop. (3) loop: destination stays null, restating ConstraintSet semantics v1 section 4. Examples 3 and 6 lose the article in their outputs; example 7 (origin stated late, afterthought not a stop) and example 8 (loop through one place, wish not a stop) are new. 4,228 prompt tokens per call against 3,531 for v1.
- **Why:** with v1, gpt-4.1-mini scored 0.225 exact match over the 17 model-set fields, with origin 0.81, destination 0.65 and stops 0.51 while every other field was 0.90 or better. Of the roughly 105 origin, destination and stop misses, most were convention gaps between the D2 gold and the ConstraintSet semantics the extractor follows, not extraction errors: 28 canonical gold names against the user's spelling or abbreviation ("Scoatiabank Arena", "the distillery", "Ripley's Aquarium"), which v1 copies by design (prohibition: never invent); about 32 generic-stop phrasing and category vocabulary ("a place for lunch" / restaurant against "lunch" / lunch, named_place against null, fuel_or_charging against ev_charging_station, which the semantics doc excludes); 7 loops whose gold destination repeats the origin where the semantics doc says null; 5 gold places the message never mentions (d2_048, 066, 068, 071, 077, all clarify items). Those are reported to WP8 and WP0. The misses that were the prompt's own: a leading "the" (16), "entrance to" (4), an origin stated mid-message (2), and 7 stops made out of wishes or afterthoughts. The ablation ran on v1 before the change so the model choice and the prompt change are not confounded.
- **Metric before -> after, D2 (102 records, gpt-4.1-mini, temperature 0, FIXTURES=record):** exact match 0.225 -> 0.255 (23 -> 26 records; 3 records fixed, none broken); lenient exact match 0.412 -> 0.451; invented-value rate 0.039 -> 0.020; origin 0.814 -> 0.873; destination 0.647 -> 0.725; stops 0.510 -> 0.549; the other 14 fields move by at most one record (prefer 0.922 both times: two records gain quiet_roads from a scenic wish, two lose it). p50 2.8 s -> 1.9 s; cost per 1,000 calls $1.67 -> $1.92. Model ablation on v1, same records: gpt-4.1-nano 0.167 exact at $0.42 and p50 1.2 s; gpt-5-mini low 0.206 at $1.84 and p50 6.0 s; gpt-5-mini medium 0.216 at $3.07 and p50 18.8 s; so gpt-4.1-mini stays the configured model. Results in eval/results/extractor.csv, every prediction next to its gold in extractor_predictions.jsonl.
- **Scoring note:** scripts/extractor_eval.py reports a lenient column next to the strict one: leading article ignored, spelling tolerance (difflib ratio >= 0.85), a null destination accepted for a loop whose gold destination repeats the origin, stops compared by query only. Strict is the headline; lenient shows how much of the remaining gap is convention. Both are computed on the same predictions, and the v1 rows were re-scored from replay fixtures, so no call was repeated.
- **Serving rule:** `EXTRACTOR_PROMPT_VERSION` selects the prompt; the default is v2 (`routechat/agents/extractor.py`). The 11 extractor replay fixtures (8 examples, 3 extra cases) were re-recorded live with `scripts/llm_fixtures.py --record --only extractor`; the 22 classifier fixtures are untouched. Test data: the four-stops case now expects "Ontario Science Centre" without the article.
